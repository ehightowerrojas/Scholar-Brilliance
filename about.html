Back to home Our Purpose

Working in Partnership for students' financial wellbeing.

Every year, more than $100 million in scholarship money goes unclaimed while most students who start an application never finish it. Scholar Brilliance exists to close that gap — helping students apply for and win the money that's already meant for them, so a degree doesn't have to start with debt.

Where this comes from

This purpose, and the principles below, are inspired by the John Lewis Partnership — the UK's largest employee-owned business — and its century-old constitution built on shared knowledge, power, and profit. They're also shaped by Earth4All and its vision of a wellbeing economy, one that grows human wellbeing and ecological health together.

We are a very young company — two people at a kitchen table, building an alpha. We don't yet have the ownership structure or the track record these ideas point toward. We're writing this page now, before we have much to show for it, because we'd rather be held to a promise made early than write one later to match whatever we've already become.

Our Principles

Three commitments, to the three groups of people our decisions affect: the people who build this company, the people and places our work touches, and the business itself.

Happier World

Contribute actively.

  • Students: we measure ourselves on completion and dollars won, not signups or engagement time. Our tools help students finish — they never manipulate students into staying.
  • Vendors: we ask the vendors we work with about their labor and environmental practices, and we choose not to work with those who won't answer.
  • Planet: we are committed to the goals of Earth4All's "Giant Leap" scenario and the wellbeing economy. As a startup, we're doing what we can with the resources we have to get off the ground responsibly. As we grow, we commit to aligning more closely with these principles — in how we account for our environmental footprint, how we price, and how we reinvest.

Happier People

Wellbeing by design.

  • Everyone who works here — member or not yet — has real quality of life: a sustainable pace, with no expectation that commitment is proven through overwork.
  • As Scholar Brilliance grows, we intend for the people who help build it to become part owners of it, not just employees of it.

Happier Business

Profit that answers to purpose.

  • We price to do four things at once: make sufficient profit to stay independent; make the platform genuinely accessible to low-income students at a discount; move toward internalizing our environmental costs as we grow; and reinvest in the business and the people who build it.
