Cookie Policy

Last updated September 2026

The short version

Scholar Brilliance does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and does not sell or share your data with advertisers. We use a small amount of essential browser storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences — nothing more.

What we actually use

Essential session storage

We use your browser's local storage (not a traditional cookie, but functionally similar and covered by this policy) to keep you signed in between visits. Without it, you'd have to log in again every time you load the site. This can't be turned off without also turning off the ability to stay logged in.

Sound and interface preferences

If you turn off in-app sound effects, we remember that choice locally in your browser so you don't have to reset it every visit.

Google Fonts

We load our typefaces from Google Fonts' servers. Google may log standard web request data (like your IP address) when your browser fetches a font file, the same as loading an image from any external server. We don't control this directly — see Google's own privacy policy for details on their end.

What we don't use

No advertising trackers, no analytics platforms that build a cross-site profile of you, no social media pixels, and no data brokers. If that ever changes, we'll update this page and ask for your consent first, not bury the change in fine print.

Managing storage yourself

You can clear your browser's local storage for this site at any time through your browser's own settings. Doing so will simply log you out — it won't affect your account or any data saved to it.
