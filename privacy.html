Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Before you use this in production: this page is a plain-language starting template, not a substitute for legal advice. Because Scholar Brilliance is used by high school students — often through their school — it likely touches laws like FERPA (student education records) and, depending on your users' ages and how accounts are created, COPPA (children under 13). Have an attorney familiar with education technology and student privacy review and adapt this before real students use the product.

1. What this covers

This policy describes what information Scholar Brilliance collects, how it's used, and who can see it, for students and school staff using the platform.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email address, password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text), and whether you're a student or school staff member.

Scholarship and application data: scholarships you save or track, their status and deadlines, essays you write, and outcomes you record (submitted, won, not selected).

School association: if you join using a referral code from your school, your account is linked to that school's staff for progress-tracking purposes.

Usage and gamification data: achievement badges earned, points, and (if you choose to appear on it) your name and point total on your school's leaderboard.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

3. How we use this information

To provide the core service: tracking your scholarship applications, computing your progress and achievements, and — if you're connected to a school — letting your school's staff see your progress and recommend scholarships suited to your interests.

4. Who can see your information

You can always see all of your own data.

Your school's staff (if you're connected to one via a referral code) can see your application progress, essays, and interests, so they can support you — but not your account password.

Other students at your school can see your name and total points on the leaderboard, unless you turn this off in Account Settings.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Service providers

We use Supabase for account authentication and data storage, and Cloudflare for hosting. Both are standard infrastructure providers and do not use your data for their own purposes.

6. Children's privacy

Scholar Brilliance is intended for high school students. If a user is under 13, additional protections under COPPA may apply, including verifiable parental or school consent before collecting personal information. If your deployment includes users under 13, consult a qualified attorney about the specific consent mechanisms required.

7. Data security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is restricted at the database level so that, with the exception of the school-staff and leaderboard visibility described above, only you can access your own records.

8. Your choices and rights

You can edit your name and email, change your password, and turn leaderboard visibility on or off at any time in Account Settings. To request a full export or deletion of your account and data, contact us using the information below.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@scholarbrilliance.com.
