Which should I use?

Invites / CSV — best when you want an accurate, live roster: you'll always know exactly who's joined and who hasn't, and seats are reserved the moment you send the invite, not left uncapped.

Referral codes — simplest for low-friction, verbal/handout distribution, but a code can be shared beyond who you intended, so there's less certainty about who's actually using it.

⚠️ For security, deactivate referral codes that are no longer in use.
